Shuffld — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Shuffld is a Reddit app (Devvit app) that posts a daily sliding-tile picture puzzle to a subreddit and keeps a leaderboard. This page explains what data it accesses and stores.

What Shuffld accesses

When you play, Shuffld reads your Reddit username, which Reddit provides to the app so scores can be tied to your account. It does not access your private messages, your email address, or any other account information, and it does not ask for your Reddit password.

What Shuffld stores

Shuffld stores the following in Reddit's Developer Platform storage, within the subreddit where it is installed:

• Your Reddit username, your best solve time and your move count, for each day, puzzle size and image. This is what powers the leaderboard.
• A streak record: how many days in a row you have solved a puzzle, and the last day you solved one.
• Which puzzle theme each Shuffld post uses, and a counter that decides the next theme.
• A short-lived marker that prevents more than one automatic post per day.

Daily scores and streak records are deleted automatically a few days after your last solve. Shuffld does not store the content of your posts or comments, and it does not build profiles of players.

Comments and flair

If you choose Share to comments and confirm, Shuffld posts one comment on that post, containing only your puzzle, time, move count and streak. It is posted from your own account when Reddit allows that, and otherwise from the Shuffld app account, with your username in the text. It never posts anything on your behalf without that confirmation. If a moderator turns on the optional streak flair setting, Shuffld sets your flair in that community to your streak.

Sharing

Shuffld does not send any data to an external service and does not show ads. It reports gameplay events (a puzzle started, how much of the picture was in place, and whether it was solved or left) to Reddit's own Journeys analytics, part of Reddit's Developer Platform, so the developer can see how the game is played. No third-party analytics or tracking is used. All processing happens within Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit), using Reddit's own APIs. Your username and solve time are visible to other players on the leaderboard, as they are shown publicly in the post.

Images

The puzzle images are in the public domain. Where an image has a source credit, the app shows it, and tapping a credit link opens that external page only if you choose to.

Removing your data

Because scores expire automatically, your leaderboard entries disappear on their own within a few days. Uninstalling Shuffld from a subreddit stops all further activity there.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the app developer through Reddit.
