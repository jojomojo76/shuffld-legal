Shuffld — Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Shuffld is a Reddit app (Devvit app) that posts a daily sliding-tile picture puzzle to a subreddit. These terms describe what the app does and does not do.

What Shuffld does

Shuffld creates puzzle posts, each themed around a small set of public-domain artworks or photographs. Players slide tiles to reassemble a picture, and their best time for the day appears on a leaderboard. A moderator can create a post at any time from the subreddit menu ("Create a new post"), and the app can also post automatically on a schedule.

Automatic posting

By default, Shuffld creates one new puzzle post per day at about 14:00 UTC. Moderators can turn automatic posting off, or change how often it posts to every day, three times a week, twice a week, or once a week, in the app's settings. Shuffld only creates its own puzzle posts. It does not remove, approve, lock, ban, or otherwise moderate any other post or comment, and does not take any moderation action against users.

Comments and flair

Players can choose to share their result as a comment. Shuffld shows the exact text first and posts it only after they confirm, at most once per post per day, from the player's own account where Reddit allows that, and otherwise from the Shuffld app account naming the player. Moderators can also turn on an optional setting that sets players' flair in their community to their current streak; it is off by default.

Fair play

Scores are ranked by solve time. Everyone playing a given puzzle on a given day gets the same scramble. Attempts to submit false times or otherwise manipulate the leaderboard may be removed, and moderators remain responsible for their own community's rules.

Installing and uninstalling

Shuffld is installed by a subreddit's moderators. Moderators may uninstall it at any time, which stops all further automatic activity. Uninstalling does not remove puzzle posts already created; moderators can remove them like any other post.

Content

The images in Shuffld are in the public domain, and are credited in the app. Players and moderators should not treat any image or title as endorsing any person or organization.
